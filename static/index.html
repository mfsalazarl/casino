How to play

Card values: A = 1, 2–10 = face value, J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13. Suit doesn't affect value.

On your turn, do one of two things:

  • Capture — select one or more cards from your hand and one or more cards from the table whose values add up to the same total. All of those cards go to your pile. Clearing the whole table this way is a sweep, worth an extra point.
  • Place — select just one card from your hand and no table cards, to put it face up on the table instead. You can do this any time, even if a capture is available.

Once both hands are empty, you're each dealt three more cards from the talon and play continues, until the talon and every hand are empty.
